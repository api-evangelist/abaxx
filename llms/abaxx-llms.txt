# Abaxx

> Abaxx Technologies is a financial software and market-infrastructure company whose principal operating business, Abaxx Exchange, is a centrally-cleared commodity futures and derivatives exchange in Singapore focused on energy-transition commodities: Energy (LNG), Environmental (carbon), Battery Materials, and Precious Metals. Abaxx also operates Abaxx Clearing and develops SMX smart-data technology. Abaxx Technologies is publicly listed (Cboe Canada: ABXX; OTCQX: ABXXF).
